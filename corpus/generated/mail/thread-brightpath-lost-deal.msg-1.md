# BrightPath went with a competitor

Hey Daniel,

Just wanted to give you a quick update on the BrightPath Logistics deal. I got word from their procurement lead this morning that they ended up going with SessionVibe instead of Pulse. 

Their team really liked our UI and the session replay features during the demo, but at the end of the day, price was the deciding factor. SessionVibe undercut us quite a bit on annual licensing, and BrightPath's finance committee wouldn't budge on their budget caps for tooling this quarter. 

Bummer since we spent a good chunk of time tailoring those custom dashboards for them, but it is what it is. I've updated the CRM and moved them to closed-lost. Let's catch up during our usual sync tomorrow to review the rest of the pipeline.
