# Why we need to move off seat-based pricing

Hey team,

Just wanted to follow up on our ongoing pricing discussions for Pulse. I spent some more time looking over the stalled enterprise deals from last quarter, and a very clear pattern has emerged that we need to address. 

During recent procurement cycles, multiple enterprise prospects—most notably Northwind Retail—flatly refused our traditional seat-based contracts. Their argument is that with automated testing and service accounts, tying the cost of a session replay and error tracking platform to individual user seats just doesn't align with how they utilize the product at scale. 

If we want to close these larger accounts this year, we really need to pivot away from per-seat licensing and transition toward a usage-based pricing model instead. Let's sync up on this during our weekly product sync tomorrow and map out what a consumption metric would actually look like for Pulse.

Best,
Claire
