# New office parking policy

Hi everyone,

Just a quick facilities update regarding the office parking situation. Starting next week, we are rolling out a new reservation system to help manage the limited spaces in the garage more fairly across the team. 

You should have received an invite to the shared calendar tool from the building management portal. Please log in when you get a chance and review your spot preferences. If you typically commute by bike or public transit, no action is needed on your end.
