# Pulse pricing (2025 launch)

Just dropping a quick note here so we have a paper trail of how we kicked things off. When we finally pushed [[Pulse]] live, we decided to go with a straightforward seat-based model. It felt like the cleanest way to get teams onboarded without overcomplicating things early on. 

To recap for anyone catching up on the history or referencing this for future [[billing refactor]] work: Lumen Labs launched Pulse with seat-based pricing at $49 per seat per month in 2025. 

We should keep an eye on how this holds up as we start talking to larger accounts. A few folks on the [[growth team]] mentioned that some prospective customers with high user turnover might push back on per-seat costs down the road, preferring usage-based tiers instead, but $49 was definitely the right sweet spot to capture initial market interest and keep our early MRR moving in the right direction. 

Let's revisit these numbers in our next quarterly retro or once we have more usage data pulled from [[Aurora migration]] metrics. For now, this is the baseline we are working from.
