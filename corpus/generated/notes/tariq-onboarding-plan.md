# Onboarding plan — Tariq Hassan

Welcome to the team, Tariq! Glad to finally have you on board with us here at Lumen Labs. 

As we discussed during your sync yesterday, your first project assignment is the [[Aurora migration]], working directly alongside Ines Dubois. Ines has been leading the initial groundwork for this over the past few weeks, particularly regarding how it intersects with our [[Pulse]] session replay pipelines and error tracking data structures, so she’ll be your best point of contact as you get your local environment spun up.

Once you've had a chance to look over the repository, we can set up a quick coffee chat to walk through any questions you have about the codebase or our internal tooling. Welcome again to the team!
