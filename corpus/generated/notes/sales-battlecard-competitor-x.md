# Sales battlecard — Competitor X

Competitor X focuses heavily on traditional error logging and basic uptime checks, but they completely miss the UX layer. When prospective buyers mention them on discovery calls, our primary angle should be our [[unified agent]] approach that merges [[session replay]] directly with frontend [[performance analytics]]. 

Their pricing model is per-seat, which punishes growing engineering teams. In contrast, our [[usage-based billing]] aligns much better with modern engineering workflows where entire cross-functional squads need access to debugging tools without inflating software budgets.

Key weaknesses to probe during demos:
- They lack DOM mutation recording, meaning they miss critical rendering glitches and layout shifts.
- Their alerting latency often spikes past five minutes during peak traffic loads.
- Custom event tracking requires code deployments rather than tag-based configurations.

When positioning Pulse against their [[enterprise tier]], emphasize our sub-second search indexing and zero-config [[error tracking]] setup. If a prospect brings up their legacy dashboard reporting, pivot the conversation toward our customizable [[custom dashboards]] and real-time user session streams. 

Avoid getting bogged down in feature-by-feature comparison matrices. Instead, steer the conversation [[discovery framework]] toward time-to-resolution metrics and how quickly an engineer can jump from a stack trace straight into the exact user session that triggered the [[crash reporting]] event.
