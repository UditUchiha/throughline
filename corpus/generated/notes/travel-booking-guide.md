# Travel booking guide

Whenever you need to head out on the road for a customer visit, conference, or team offsite, just run your bookings through the standard company portal. Make sure you book your flights and hotels at least three weeks in advance to keep costs reasonable, especially if you are traveling during peak seasons or heading to major tech hubs. 

If you are coordinating a larger team meetup—similar to what we usually map out in [[Offsite planning]]—try to align everyone's itineraries so people arrive within a reasonable window of each other. It makes airport pickups and hotel check-ins much smoother for everyone involved. 

For ground transportation, standard rideshares or public transit are always preferred over expensive car rentals unless you are traveling with a large pile of hardware or heading out to a remote data center facility. Keep all your itemized receipts in a safe place so you can attach them to your monthly expense report [[Expense policies]] before the finance cutoff date at the end of the month. 

If you run into any weird edge cases, travel delays, or last-minute itinerary changes while you are away, just drop a message in the designated Slack channel for help. Safe travels!
