# Marketing campaign retro — Q4 2025

Overall, the Q4 push felt a lot smoother than our mid-year [[lead generation]] sprint, mostly because we actually had [[design assets]] ready before the first batch of ads went live. We tried out three different [[audience segments]] for the new [[explainer video]], and the middle-tier developer persona definitely outperformed the enterprise-focused one. 

That said, coordination between [[content production]] and the [[growth team]] could still use some work. Halfway through November, we noticed a few typos in the primary landing page copy that had been sitting there for over a week. Nobody flagged it because everyone assumed someone else owned the [[copywriting review]] loop. 

For the next [[budget cycle]], we should probably allocate more resources toward [[community events]] rather than purely pouring money into paid search. The conversion rates from the [[developer meetup]] sponsorships were surprisingly solid, even if they are harder to attribute directly in [[analytics dashboards]]. Let’s make sure we schedule a proper [[strategy sync]] before diving into any new [[collaboration tools]] or agency pitches for the upcoming quarter.
