# New-hire buddy program

Starting as an engineer at Lumen Labs can feel a bit like drinking from a firehose, especially when you are trying to wrap your head around our core codebase and [[Pulse]] architecture all at once. To help ease the transition, we set up the buddy program so every newcomer has a dedicated point of contact during their first month.

Your buddy is not your manager, which is the best part. It is an informal resource for all the mundane questions you don't want to bug a lead about. Where do we keep the best documentation? How do I set up my local environment without breaking everything? Who is the right person to ping when staging acts up? That sort of thing.

Buddies usually schedule a casual check-in twice a week during your first month, maybe grab coffee or jump on a quick video call if you are working remotely. They will also help you review your first couple of pull requests so you can get familiar with our code standards and review culture. 

We try to pair folks up across different squads to help you build out your internal network beyond your immediate team. If you are joining us soon and want to suggest a buddy preference, just drop a note in the [[onboarding]] channel on Slack. We are always tweaking this process, so feedback from recent hires is super welcome.
