# Customer success playbook

Maintaining healthy long-term accounts starts with staying on top of product adoption metrics and regular check-ins. Whenever a team onboard via [[onboarding workflows]] goes live with our tracking scripts, we want to ensure [[customer health scoring]] reflects their actual engagement within the first fourteen days.

Our primary focus is monitoring weekly active users, error resolution velocity, and overall dashboard usage trends. If an account's activity drops significantly compared to their historical baseline, it usually signals friction with a recent deployment or internal team turnover. 

For enterprise accounts, we schedule quarterly business reviews to align [[Pulse]] capabilities with their engineering roadmaps. These sessions are great opportunities to review performance analytics trends, share upcoming feature previews, and gather direct product feedback. 

Day-to-day engagement relies heavily on our shared communication channels. When critical issues or urgent troubleshooting requests pop up, response time directly correlates with retention. Standard support tickets should be triaged through our usual internal escalation paths, while CSMs handle strategic relationship management. 

Remember to log all major account updates and expansion opportunities in the CRM [[CRM integration]] so the entire product and sales team stays aligned. Keeping these notes detailed helps immensely when preparing for renewals or introducing new modules like [[session replay enhancements]].
