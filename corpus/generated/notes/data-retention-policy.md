# Data retention policy

As we scale our infrastructure and handle increasing ingestion volumes across our [[infrastructure]] stack, establishing clear guidelines around [[data retention]] is critical for maintaining performance and managing [[storage costs]]. This note outlines the baseline principles for how long we keep [[raw logs]], [[processed metrics]], and [[debug artifacts]] in our [[ClickHouse]] clusters and [[object storage]].

By default, high-frequency telemetry data generated by our [[sdk]] and [[collector]] services is retained for a rolling window of 90 days. After this period, [[raw event streams]] are automatically [[aggregated]] into hourly and daily summaries to save [[disk space]] while preserving historical trend visibility for our [[analytics dashboard]]. 

Debug payloads, including [[stack traces]] and [[network payloads]] captured during exception handling, are kept for a shorter duration of 30 days unless explicitly [[pinned]] or [[bookmarked]] by a team member investigating an active [[incident]]. [[Customer exports]] and [[temporary backups]] stored in [[S3 buckets]] follow a strict 14 [[retention period]] before being purged by our [[lifecycle scripts]].

Compliance-related audit logs and [[access records]] are subject to a separate [[governance schedule]] managed alongside our [[security review]] policies. If any team needs temporary extensions for [[debugging]] or [[load testing]], requests should be routed through the [[platform channel]] for approval.
