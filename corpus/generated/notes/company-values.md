# Company values

At Lumen Labs, our culture has always been centered around curiosity, ownership, and moving with a healthy sense of urgency. We are a tight-knit team, and how we build [[Pulse]] is just as important as the code we ship every week. 

First and foremost, we believe in radical transparency. Whether we are discussing product roadmaps or reviewing infrastructure costs, open communication keeps everyone aligned. Nobody likes working in a silo, so we keep our [[Team syncs]] conversational and make sure [[Engineering guidelines]] are accessible to everyone on the team, from senior architects to our newest hires.

We also champion developer autonomy. Trust is foundational here; we hire smart people and give them the space to execute without micromanagement. If someone wants to prototype a new feature or experiment with a different approach to [[Performance analytics]], they are encouraged to run with it, test it thoroughly, and share what they learn with the rest of the crew. Mistakes are treated as learning opportunities rather than failures, provided we are open about what went wrong and how to fix it.

Finally, we keep our users at the center of everything. We spend a lot of time talking to engineering teams who rely on our platform day in and day out. Their pain points inform our backlog, and our shared obsession with reducing friction and latency drives us forward. 

As we grow [[Lumen Labs]] beyond thirty people, maintaining this collaborative and builder-first ethos is our highest priority.
