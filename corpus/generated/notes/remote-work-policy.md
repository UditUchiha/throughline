# Remote work policy

As we continue to grow our team across different time zones, maintaining a healthy balance between focused independent work and collaborative engineering is essential. This [[Remote work policy]] outlines our expectations for hybrid and distributed team members.

Core working hours overlap roughly four hours per day to ensure smooth communication across Slack and during scheduled syncs. Outside of those core hours, everyone has flexibility to structure their days around personal schedules, family commitments, or deep-work blocks. 

When working from home, please ensure you have a reliable internet connection and a reasonably quiet space for video calls. If you plan to work [[Working from abroad|from a different country]] for an extended period, coordinate with HR and your tech lead ahead of time to handle any tax or security compliance basics. 

Equipment budgets cover standard peripheral upgrades and ergonomic home office setups through our internal [[IT procurement]] request form. For those who prefer an in-person environment, the shared downtown co-working space remains open daily, complete with coffee, snacks, and whiteboard rooms for spontaneous brainstorming sessions. 

We review these guidelines periodically during team retrospectives to adapt as our engineering squad scales. If you have any suggestions or encounter friction with asynchronous workflows, feel free to drop a note in the [[People Ops]] channel.
