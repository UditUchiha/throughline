# Brand style guide

When updating the [[Design System]] or building out new UI components for [[Pulse]], keeping our visual identity consistent is key. This quick reference covers our core brand guidelines for everyday use.

### Color Palette
Our primary brand color remains deep obsidian, paired with high-contrast electric indigo for interactive elements and primary buttons. Neutral grays are used for structural borders and background cards, ensuring high legibility in both light and dark modes. Avoid introducing saturated accent colors outside of our standard status indicators (success green, warning amber, error red).

### Typography
We use Inter across all product interfaces and marketing materials for its exceptional readability at small sizes. Headings should generally be semi-bold, while body text stays at regular weight. Ensure that line heights are kept generous to prevent dense blocks of text in our analytics dashboards and error logs.

### Logo Usage
The primary logo must always have adequate breathing room—at least the height of the logomark on all sides. Do not stretch, skew, or alter the color of the logo. For dark backgrounds, use the full-white variant; for light surfaces, stick to the solid dark version. Never place the logo over busy photographic backgrounds without a solid backing or proper dimming overlay.

### Tone and Voice
When writing error messages, tooltips, or empty states, aim for a voice that is direct, helpful, and concise. We want to guide users efficiently without unnecessary fluff or overly casual slang. If an exception occurs, clearly state what went wrong and provide a direct path toward remediation, linking out to our [[Documentation]] whenever possible.

Reach out to the [[Design Team]] if you need vector assets or have questions about custom component states.
