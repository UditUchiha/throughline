# Q1 2026 OKRs

Happy Friday team! As we kick off the new year, I wanted to drop a quick summary of where our focus lies for the upcoming quarter. We’ve all been moving fast on [[Pulse]] development, and Q1 is all about balancing core reliability with scaling our infrastructure to handle our growing customer base.

Our primary company-wide theme this quarter is "Deepening Observability." Rather than spreading ourselves too thin across a dozen shiny features, the leadership team wants us to double down on making our core session replay and error tracking loops buttery smooth. If an engineer or product manager is working on a ticket, it should directly tie back to either reducing ingestion latency or improving query speeds in the dashboard.

On the infrastructure side [[Aurora migration]], the platform guild is continuing their work behind the scenes. No immediate action needed from feature squads yet, but expect some RFCs coming down the pipe regarding database partitioning and caching strategies. Meanwhile, design and frontend are syncing up on a massive accessibility sweep across the entire web app. 

As always, let's keep communication transparent in our channels. If anyone feels like their squad's sprint goals are drifting away from these broader themes, ping your tech lead or grab some time on my calendar. Let’s make Q1 our best quarter yet!
