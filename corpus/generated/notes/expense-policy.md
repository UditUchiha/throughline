# Expense policy

As we continue to grow the team here at Lumen Labs, it is important to keep our everyday spending straightforward and transparent. Whether you are grabbing lunch during an in-person sync or picking up a new desk accessory to improve your local setup, this quick overview covers the general guidelines for expensing items.

For day-to-day team meals or individual working lunches, please use good judgment. We do not enforce strict per-diem limits, but standard team norms apply. If you are hosting a client dinner or organizing a broader team get-together—perhaps to celebrate a milestone related to [[Pulse]] or our ongoing infrastructure work—make sure to get a quick sign-off from your manager beforehand. 

Travel and transit expenses should be booked through our preferred corporate channels whenever possible. Keep all itemized receipts, as our finance team needs them to process reimbursements smoothly through the expense portal. Screenshots of email confirmations or digital receipts are totally fine; just avoid submitting credit card statements alone, as itemization is required for bookkeeping.

If you need to purchase software licenses, subscriptions, or hardware upgrades to support your work, check with [[IT support]] first. We often have enterprise discounts or pre-vouched vendor pathways that make procurement much faster. For anything unusual that is not explicitly covered by these general guidelines, just drop a message in the general chat channels or check in directly with finance. Thanks for helping us keep operations running smoothly!
