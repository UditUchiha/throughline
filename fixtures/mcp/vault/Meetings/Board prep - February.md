# Board prep — February

My own prep, written before the session. What I intend to say, and what I intend
not to be talked out of.

## Say plainly

- The launch slipped. The reason is a dependency we scoped badly, not a team
  that under-delivered.
- We caught it in a load test, which is the system working. Late, but working.
- The new date has a load test *before* it.

## Do not say

- Anything with the shape "we are confident". Confidence is not a plan and the
  board has heard it from me once already this quarter.
- Any number I have not personally traced to something Amara or Ravi wrote down.

## What I expect to be asked

1. "Is this the last slip?" — the honest answer is that I cannot promise that,
   and the useful answer is what would have to be true for it to be.
2. "What does this cost us with Northwind?" — Sofia has the account view; I
   should not improvise it.
3. Something about hiring, because there is always something about hiring.
